What's out of scope, and why

The certification's own 5 dedicated prep modules and its exam guide (objectives, weightings) are hosted on the Claude Partner Network (CPN) academy — gated behind partner login. Per the certifications FAQ: "Certification is available to people at Claude Partner Network organizations. Registration requires a partner email address on a recognized company domain."

Next step, beyond this course Actually sitting the exam requires CPN membership. Apply (free, per the FAQ) at claude.com/partners → the CPN partner application form. The site's language ("partner organizations," "a recognized company domain") suggests this is org-oriented; individual eligibility isn't stated outright, so applying is the way to find out.

Exam mechanics, for later reference

Once CPN-eligible: Claude Certified Developer – Foundations costs $125 (partner-tier discounts apply), is Pearson-proctored (online or test center) since 2026-06-30, 120 minutes, multiple-choice/scenario-based. Retake waits: 14 days after the 1st fail, 30 after the 2nd, 90 after the 3rd — max 4 attempts per rolling 12 months.
