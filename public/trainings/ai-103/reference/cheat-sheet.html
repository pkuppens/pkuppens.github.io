Classic pairing traps
  • SLM (narrow, cheap, fast) vs. LLM (broad, capable) — pick by task shape, not by default reflex.
  • RAG (grounds at inference time, always current) vs. fine-tuning (changes the weights, goes stale) — don't confuse "add data" with "retrain".
  • Single-task Content Understanding (one job) vs. pro-mode (several coordinated extractions in one pass).
  • Managed identity (authenticates keylessly) vs. private networking (hides traffic from the internet) — two different D1 security controls, often both needed together.
  • Vector search (embedding similarity) vs. semantic search (relevance re-ranking) vs. hybrid search (keyword + vector combined).
  • Approval workflow (human gates a specific action) vs. tool-access control (allow-list of what an agent may call at all).
  • Speech recognition (audio→text) vs. speech synthesis (text→audio) vs. speech translation (crosses languages, either direction).
  • Fabrication evaluation (did it invent facts?) vs. safety evaluation (is the content harmful?) — both named evaluation dimensions, testing different things.
