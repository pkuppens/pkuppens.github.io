The shape of the exam

AI-103 has five domains. The weighting tells you where to spend cram time:

DomainWeightWhat it is
D1 Plan and manage an Azure AI solution25–30%Choosing services, infrastructure & deployment setup, monitoring/security, responsible-AI governance.
D2 Implement generative AI and agentic solutions30–35%The heaviest domain: Foundry SDK, RAG, agents, orchestration, evaluation, optimization.
D3 Implement computer vision solutions10–15%Image/video generation & editing, multimodal understanding, visual responsible AI.
D4 Implement text analysis solutions10–15%Language-model text analysis, sentiment/entities/translation, speech-to-text and text-to-speech.
D5 Implement information extraction solutions10–15%Retrieval & grounding pipelines, Content Understanding, document extraction.

Pass mark is 700/1000, 120 minutes, proctored. Full details in the Exam Blueprint; vocabulary in the Glossary; last-pass recap in the Cheat Sheet.

Your edge D2 (generative AI + agents) is nearly a third of the exam and is exactly what you already build day to day — the work is learning Microsoft Foundry's names for concepts you already know. D1's governance/security vocabulary and D3/D4/D5's service-name matching are the parts most worth deliberate drilling.
